What Uptick shows

Uptick tracks your watchlist against a live (or simulated) price feed. Each ticker's close price is plotted against a configurable simple moving average (SMA), with the gap between the two shown as a histogram underneath — a quick read on whether a ticker is running hot above its own trend or cooling off below it.

The four chips in the top-right are quick market context — ^IXIC (Nasdaq Composite), NQ=F (Nasdaq futures), CL=F (crude oil futures), and ^VIX — shown at a glance, same as any watchlist ticker under the hood.

Symbol groups

The row of chips above the ticker bar are named snapshots of which symbols are currently selected — not separate watchlists. Your actual watchlist (the full ticker roster) is never touched by any of this.

  • Click a group to switch to it — replaces your current selection outright
  • Save group saves whatever's currently checked under a name you type
  • Saving under an existing name updates that group instead of creating a duplicate — saving under a new name instead is a quick way to clone one group into another
  • The small on a chip deletes that saved group only — your watchlist symbols aren't affected

Close

The last traded price at each interval, plotted as the black line.

SMA

SMA(n) = average of the last n closes

The blue line — the reference trend the delta bars measure against. n is set in Settings.

Delta bars

Delta = Close − SMA

Green when price is running above its SMA, red when below. Scaled small on purpose so it reads as a strip, not a second chart.

Hourly marks

Solid, light grey, unlabeled vertical lines at 10am–3pm on the Today chart — a quiet reference grid for the regular trading session, distinct from the dashed pink Open/Close lines at 9:30/4:00.

Combined

One combined line: the average % change (from the start of the window) across every ticker currently selected — a quick read on how the whole selected basket is moving together, not any single name.

Compare

Every selected ticker side by side, each with its own Close/SMA/delta chart, same as the Combined chart but per-ticker.

Table

For the current timeframe window: the price at the start of the chart, the price at the end, and the $ / % delta between them — not a day-over-day number.

Research

A one-off symbol lookup, separate from your watchlist — search any symbol, see its chart at any timeframe, without adding it or affecting your selected tickers. Purely for research: there's no path from here to your real watchlist. Ticker selection and symbol groups both grey out and have no effect while this view is active. Not saved between sessions — the searched symbol resets on reload.

Picking a past date

The calendar icon next to Today opens a date picker. Pick a date, and every timeframe — Today, 10D, 30D, and so on — treats that date as "today": the same window it would normally show, just anchored to a different day.

Click the Today label (not the calendar icon) to return to live, or just wait — after 60 minutes with no clicks anywhere in the app, it resets on its own.

While browsing a past date:

  • Buy/Sell and Last 6H — disabled (greyed out, not just click-rejected)
  • Chart auto-refresh — paused, since history doesn't change
  • Held-position dot / $/% gain — hidden, since those reflect your current position, not the picked date
  • Account/positions — stay fully live regardless, since your real balance is a current-day fact either way

5-minute intraday data ("Today") isn't retained indefinitely by most data providers — a date far enough in the past may come back empty even though 10D/30D/etc. for that same date work fine. That's a data availability limit, not a bug.

Data source

Switch between simulated sample data (for building/demoing) and your real backend. Sample mode regenerates a random-walk series locally; API mode calls the endpoints below. Symbol data and account data can point at two entirely different services.

Purpose Endpoint
Series GET {activeSymbolSourceUrl}/{symbol}/{timeframe}/{startISO}/{endISO}
Account GET {accountBaseUrl}/v2/account
Positions GET {accountBaseUrl}/v2/positions
Buy POST {accountBaseUrl}/v2/orders
Sell DELETE {accountBaseUrl}/v2/positions/{symbol}?percentage=100
Liquidate DELETE {accountBaseUrl}/v2/positions

Symbol data supports a Primary and a Backup source with the same contract — switch between them from a Settings toggle if one is having issues, without losing either URL.

{timeframe} is 5Min for Today or 1D for 10D/30D. {startISO}/{endISO} are full ISO timestamps; end always defaults to the end of the current calendar day.

Expected series response: {"bars":{"SYMBOL":[{"o":n,"h":n,"l":n,"c":n,"t":"ISO string","e":epochMs,"v":n}, ...]}} — the symbol key sits under a top-level bars object. Only t and c are used today; SMA is computed client-side from c.

This is Alpaca's own API shape throughout (/v2/account, /v2/positions, /v2/orders). Every request sends your API key/secret as APCA-API-KEY-ID and APCA-API-SECRET-KEY headers — Alpaca's actual header names, not a generic placeholder. Stored only in this browser's local storage, never written into the app's source.

Today & Positions

Today comes from the account resource's equity and last_equity fields (today's change = equity − last_equity) — the account-level number, not derived from individual positions.

Positions comes from the positions resource: an array, one entry per open position, each with cost_basis and unrealized_pl (numeric strings). Aggregated dollar-weighted across every returned position, not averaged per-position.

Buy/Sell buttons

Sit at the bottom of every Compare tile, icon-only — hover for a tooltip.

  • Grey — buy $100
  • Teal — buy $1,000
  • Green — buy $10,000
  • Red — sell (full position)

The amount is editable via a prompt before confirming; Sell is gated behind a confirm dialog. Neither fires without that explicit step, and neither actually places an order until Live API mode is fully configured — otherwise clicking opens Settings instead.

Liquidate account

The small icon in the top-right corner of the Positions card — not per-symbol, exists once. It closes every open position immediately, gated behind a confirm dialog.

Visual cues

Five-minute highlight — the Today card gets a steady blue border for the full minute at each :00/:05/:10/... mark, while the market's live. Deliberately not a flash or pulse — the point is a reliable, easy-to-notice checkpoint cue, not another source of reactive urgency.

Loss alert — a red hue appears on the POSITIONS card, and independently on each row and the TOTAL in the Positions table, whenever that figure's % change drops below your configured threshold (default -0.5%, adjustable in Settings). Runs on the account refresh cadence, so it stays current around the clock, not just during trading hours.
