Help

Logging a catch

Click anywhere on the Waypoints map, or tap the pin icon in the header, to log a catch there. Every field has a default so you can log one fast — species defaults to "Fish," the time defaults to now — and it grabs the current wind, temperature, pressure, and cloud cover for that exact spot automatically. "Add photo" opens your camera on a phone (or a file picker on desktop) and shrinks the photo down before saving it, so it doesn't eat much storage. If the photo has location and time info attached (common for photos shared from someone else's phone with location on), picking it when logging a new catch will automatically set the pin's location and catch time from the photo, and pull the weather for that exact date instead of right now.

Editing, deleting, or sharing a catch

Click any pin you've added to reopen it, change anything, or delete it. From there, Share sends the catch's details (and photo, if it has one) through your device's normal share sheet — Messages, Mail, wherever — or copies the details to your clipboard if sharing isn't available. The 5 sample pins you see before you've logged anything are just placeholders — they disappear the moment you add your first real one.

Weather

The Wind / Temp / Baro / Clouds chips in the header show current conditions at your location — click any chip to refresh it. The Weather tab (next to Waypoints, above the map) gives you Windy's full interactive map and forecast table, including radar, for browsing conditions in more depth.

Your location

BITEMAP uses your live position when you allow it, falls back to a home location you can set in Settings, and falls back again to a default sample location if neither is available — so there's always something sensible on screen.

Moving data between devices

There's no account or server yet, so each device keeps its own waypoints (and photos) in this browser. Use Export (bottom of the page) to copy your waypoint data to the clipboard, paste it somewhere to carry over (a note app works fine), then paste it into Import on another device. Import replaces that device's waypoints with whatever you paste, so it also carries over any deleting you've done. Photos don't travel with Export/Import — they stay only on the device you took them on until there's a real backend to sync them.
